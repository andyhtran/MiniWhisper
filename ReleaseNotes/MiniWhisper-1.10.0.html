MiniWhisper 1.10.0

Fn key combinations can no longer be bound

Shortcuts that combine Fn with another key — such as Fn+W — no longer work. macOS offers no way to register them without also swallowing the plain key everywhere, which would mean binding Fn+W stopped W from reaching any app at all.

If you had one, an orange warning marker now appears next to it in the menu bar and in Settings. Click the shortcut and record a new one to fix it.

  • Fn (Globe) on its own is unaffected.
  • Shortcuts using arrow keys or function keys are unaffected and carry over automatically.
